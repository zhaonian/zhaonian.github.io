Privacy policy · Effective November 19, 2026

Aster, operated by Zhaonian Luan, helps you save and organize links, screenshots, notes, and ideas.

Your library

Saved content and collections are stored locally in Aster’s shared app container on your device. Aster has no account system or cloud-sync service. Apple may include app data in device backups according to your settings. You can delete saved items or export their text and details; image binaries are not included in JSON exports. Deleting Aster removes its local app data.

Web content and screenshots

When you save or revisit a web link, Aster requests accessible metadata, structured content, and images from that site. The site receives ordinary network request information, including your IP address. Private or restricted content may be unavailable. Aster does not bypass access controls. Screenshot text recognition and Apple Intelligence processing run on device. No saved content is sent to a remote AI provider in this version.

For RedNote links, Aster uses a temporary browser session on your device to load the public post and its page resources, then reads the post title and caption. Comments and navigation are excluded. This session does not reuse your Safari or Chrome cookies, does not sign in, and is discarded after reading or a timeout. Your notes and screenshots are not sent to RedNote.

Sharing

A shared Aster card contains its title, source URL, summary, category, and extracted details. Your private notes, favorites, collection membership, and local photos are excluded. Check the card before sharing. A library export includes your personal notes, so share exports carefully.

Subscriptions

Apple processes payment. RevenueCat receives purchase history and an anonymous app user identifier to validate, maintain, restore, and analyze subscriptions. Aster does not receive payment card details or send saved links, screenshots, notes, or searches to RevenueCat. There are no advertising trackers or third-party usage analytics SDKs. Purchase information may be retained as needed for subscription management, fraud prevention, and legal obligations.

See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Your choices

You can use the free plan without a purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Photos are selected through Apple’s system photo picker. Use Settings → Restore purchases to recover an active subscription. Existing saved items remain readable after cancellation.

Website and support

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information to deliver and secure the site. The pages contain no analytics scripts. See GitHub’s privacy statement. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send. Please avoid including private saved content or payment details.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions or access, correction, or deletion requests, email lzn0830@gmail.com. We may need enough information to locate a purchase record. We cannot access your on-device library. We update this policy when practices change; the effective date identifies this version.
