Your memories stay yours.

Privacy policy · Effective November 17, 2026

Poke helps you review and organize trip photos on your iPhone. This policy explains how Poke, operated by Zhaonian Luan, uses information.

Photos and albums

Photo content, capture locations, album names, and review decisions are processed on your device. Poke does not upload your photo library to its own servers, Google Analytics, or RevenueCat. Apple Photos manages iCloud downloads and confirms deletions. Keepers remain in Apple Photos; creating or choosing an album is optional and does not create extra photo copies. You can choose limited Photos access or change access in iOS Settings.

Information on your device

Drafts, onboarding progress, daily review counts and asset identifiers, analytics preferences, review-prompt progress, reminder preferences, and location-name caches are stored locally. Location search and place-name lookups use Apple services under Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the app removes its local app data; it does not delete photos or albums in Apple Photos.

Optional memory reminders

Reminders are off until you enable them. They are scheduled locally on your phone, without a notification server or collection of a push notification token. Your reminder schedule and last review time stay on your device. Notifications contain no photo or album details, and have no sound or badge. Choose weekly or every two weeks, change the day and time, or turn them off in Poke → Your Poke → Memory reminders. Finishing a review postpones the next reminder; after four reminders without a visit, reminders pause until you open Poke again.

Optional usage analytics

Usage analytics is off until you choose to share it. If enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase receives app activity such as screen visits, welcome steps, review starts and completions, album destination type (none, new, or existing), aggregate photo counts, and purchase outcomes. It also receives a random app-instance identifier, device and app information, and approximate location derived from your internet connection.

We use this information to understand which features work well and improve Poke. Photo content, photo locations, album or trip names, asset identifiers, search text, and your email address are not included in these analytics events. Poke does not collect advertising identifiers or vendor identifiers for analytics, use Google Signals, or use this information for personalized advertising. Granular location and device data collection is disabled in our Analytics property.

Enable or disable sharing anytime in Poke → Your Poke → Share usage analytics. Turning it off stops future analytics collection and resets the local analytics identifier. It does not remove data already sent. Analytics event and user data is retained for up to 14 months without extending that period on new activity; aggregated reports may remain longer. Google processes this data on its infrastructure, which may be outside your country. See Google's privacy policy and Firebase privacy information.

Subscriptions

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat receives purchase information and an app user identifier to validate, maintain, and restore Poke Plus access. Poke does not receive your payment card details. Subscription processing is necessary for purchases and is separate from optional usage analytics. RevenueCat may retain purchase information as needed for subscription management and legal obligations. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Your choices and requests

You can use Poke without sharing usage analytics. You can change photo access in iOS Settings, manage subscriptions through your Apple Account, and clear app data by deleting the app. To ask about access, correction, or deletion of information, email lzn0830@gmail.com or visit Poke Support. We may need information sufficient to locate the relevant app-instance or purchase record. We cannot identify your photos or local drafts on our servers because we do not receive them.

App Store review requests

After five completed single-photo decisions, Poke may ask Apple to display its native review prompt at a quiet moment. A swipe count and whether Poke has requested a review stay on your device. Poke does not receive your star rating or review response from this prompt. Apple controls the prompt and handles any review you choose to submit under its own policies.

Website hosting

This policy and the terms page are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including your IP address, to deliver and secure these pages. These pages contain no analytics scripts. See GitHub’s privacy statement. The Poke marketing and support site is hosted by Firebase Hosting and contains no analytics tag.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when Poke's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
