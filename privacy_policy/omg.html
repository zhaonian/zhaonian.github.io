Privacy Policy · OMG (Offline Multipeer Games)

Effective Date: August 16, 2026
Responsible Party: Zhaonian Luan

OMG (Offline Multipeer Games) is designed from the ground up for private, 100% offline local multiplayer gaming between nearby devices. The app uses Apple's native Multipeer Connectivity framework to enable device-to-device gameplay without using external servers, cloud services, or public internet networks.

Summary

  • Zero Remote Collection: No accounts, no cloud servers, no remote analytics, and no advertising trackers.
  • Local Peer-to-Peer Transmission: Game state and party lobby data are transmitted directly between nearby devices via Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.
  • No Data Retention on Remote Servers: No user data, device identifiers, or gameplay history are ever transmitted to or stored by the developer.
  • Local Storage Only: Player nickname preferences and selected avatar avatars remain stored locally on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, monitor, track, or share any personal information, location history, unique device identifiers (IDFA/IDFV), or contact details. OMG operates entirely offline and maintains no external databases or telemetry servers.

Device Permissions and Network Usage

  • Local Network & Bluetooth: Used strictly to discover and establish encrypted peer-to-peer connections with nearby devices hosting or joining a game lobby. Traffic remains 100% local between participating devices.
  • Nearby Devices Permission: Used to advertise and discover local Multipeer party rooms ("Neko Camp").

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party. OMG contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or external tracking scripts.

Children's Privacy

OMG is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect future app enhancements or legal compliance. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
