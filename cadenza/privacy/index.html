Privacy policy.

Updated November 19, 2026

Cadenza is developed by Zhaonian Luan. This policy describes the information used by the iPhone and iPad app and this support website. Contact lzn0830@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Cadenza is in development. Live composition and subscriptions are currently disabled. The sections below explain how those features are designed to work when enabled. The bundled piano preview plays locally.

What stays on your device.

Your saved audio, titles, musical notes, favorites, and library metadata are stored in the app’s local files. Your sign-in session and unfinished request identifier are kept in the iOS Keychain. Cadenza does not sync your library between devices. Device backups may include app files according to your Apple backup settings.

Cadenza does not request access to your microphone, contacts, photos, or precise location. It does not use advertising identifiers or advertising trackers.

When you sign in.

Sign in with Apple supplies an identity token, a one-time authorization code, and an app-specific Apple account identifier. The server validates them to create a Cadenza account. It stores a hashed Apple identifier, a random Cadenza account identifier, session-token hashes, and an encrypted Apple refresh token needed to revoke authorization when the account is deleted. Cadenza does not request your name or email address from Apple.

When you compose.

After you enable permission to send prompts, your musical description, mood, ensemble, tempo, and approximate duration are sent through Cadenza’s server to Google Lyria. Generated audio returns through the server to your device. Your chosen library title is not included in the generation prompt.

The server keeps request identifiers, prompt hashes, timestamps, completion status, and account allowance records to deliver recordings, recover interrupted downloads, and prevent duplicate requests. Temporary audio recovery copies are normally removed after seven days. The Cadenza server does not store the full text of your musical prompt in its database.

Google processes the prompt and output under its Gemini API terms and privacy policy. Unpaid API services may use inputs and outputs to improve Google’s products and may involve human review. Paid API services have different data-use terms. Avoid sensitive, confidential, or personal information in musical descriptions. The current free development preview used a generic musical prompt, not personal user content.

Subscriptions.

Apple processes payments. Cadenza uses RevenueCat to validate purchases and provide subscription access. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous app user identifier, purchase and receipt information, subscription status, and technical information such as app and operating-system versions. An anonymous RevenueCat identifier may be created before you sign in; it is associated with your Cadenza identifier when you sign in. Cadenza does not receive your payment-card number.

See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy for their practices.

Hosting, security, and support.

Cloudflare hosts the Cadenza API and database. Network providers process IP addresses and request information to deliver and protect the service. The app server temporarily uses a hash of an IP address to limit sign-in requests. Our support website is hosted with GitHub Pages; we do not add analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or advertising to these pages.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and resolve the issue. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States.

Retention and deletion.

Local recordings remain until you delete them or remove the app. Exported copies and device backups are under your control. Sessions expire after 30 days. Server account and allowance records remain while your account exists; temporary security records expire automatically. An anonymous generation ledger, containing request identifiers and timestamps without your account identifier or prompt, remains to track total service usage.

Use Settings → Delete account to remove the server account, sessions, generation records, and temporary audio. The server also requests Apple authorization revocation and RevenueCat customer deletion. If a provider is unavailable, a minimal cleanup record is retained and retried; the encrypted Apple token is erased after revocation, and the cleanup record is erased after provider cleanup finishes. Recreating an account may be temporarily unavailable while this finishes.

Account deletion leaves local recordings on the device and does not cancel a subscription. Cancel separately in Apple Account subscription settings. Apple and other providers may retain transaction or security records under their own policies and legal obligations. Contact support if you cannot access the in-app deletion control or need help with a data request.

Your choices.

You can turn off Allow sending musical prompts to Google in Settings at any time to stop new prompt submissions. This does not withdraw a request already sent. You can sign out, delete local recordings, export your music, manage your subscription, or request account deletion separately. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal information. Contact us to make a request; we may need to verify account ownership.

Age and changes.

Cadenza’s cloud composition service is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information.

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date above identifies the latest revision; material changes to data handling will be disclosed in the app before the affected feature is used.
