A personal listening room

Music, made personal.

A piano nocturne for a quiet evening. A string quartet for a new beginning. Original classical music, shaped by your imagination.

Cadenza is a native iPhone and iPad app inspired by the expressive worlds of Beethoven, Chopin, Debussy, and Bach. Choose a mood and ensemble, give your piece a little direction, and keep your recordings in a personal music library.

Cadenza is in a limited internal beta. Apple and Google sign-in, cloud composition, private libraries, and listening links are enabled for testing. Google sign-in is limited to approved test accounts; Facebook sign-in is limited to registered Meta app testers; public access remains unavailable. Subscription testing uses Apple’s sandbox; no public TestFlight or App Store download is available yet.

A little help, when you need it.

For playback, account, purchase, or privacy questions, contact the developer. Include your app version, device model, and a short description of what happened. Please do not send passwords, Apple verification codes, payment-card details, or private musical prompts.

Compose, then carry on.

Once Cadenza confirms your composition is in the cloud, you can leave or close the app. Your music continues taking shape in the cloud. Reopen Cadenza with an internet connection to check progress and download the finished piece. If your connection drops during submission, retry the saved request to avoid creating a duplicate. You can allow completion notifications when you first tap Generate. If permission is granted, Cadenza can send an alert when your piece is ready; notification delivery depends on your device settings and connectivity.

Your recordings, close at hand.

Saved pieces play offline. You can rename, favorite, delete, or export a recording from your library. New compositions are saved on your device and in a private cloud library. Sign in with the same provider to restore the collection on another device and download pieces for offline listening. During the beta, keep an exported copy of music you want to preserve before removing the app or replacing your device.

AI compositions are new generated recordings, not performances by the named composers. Generated music may resemble other outputs, and style or duration requests may not be followed exactly.

A little music, shared.

A listening link lets someone open your piece and play it in a browser without installing Cadenza or signing in. New recordings stay private. Creating a listening link makes that piece accessible to people who have the link; you can stop sharing from the app. Your personal musical note stays private.

Listen includes studio selections, a community feed, and search. Listener’s Choice ranks public pieces by real likes. Publishing a piece makes it discoverable in Listen and accessible by browser link; making it private removes it from public discovery and disables its links. The current app focuses on individual pieces and favorites.

About Cadenza Patron.

Patron is planned to include 40 compositions per calendar month, available through monthly or annual subscriptions. Both plans use the same monthly allowance, which refreshes on the first day of the month at 00:00 UTC; unused compositions do not roll over. Eligible new subscribers can receive a three-day free trial with up to three compositions, shared with any complimentary compositions already used. Starting a trial does not reset used credits. The internal beta remains subject to service capacity.

When subscriptions become available, the app will display the localized price and billing period before you confirm a purchase with Apple. Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled in Apple Account settings. Cancellation stops future renewals; access continues through the paid period. Saved recordings stay on your device after cancellation.

Use Settings → Restore purchases in Cadenza with the Apple Account used to purchase. Manage or cancel through Apple subscriptions. For refunds, visit Apple’s Report a Problem.

Privacy, with a clear choice.

Sign in to enter Cadenza after the introduction. Composing also requires permission to send your musical description for cloud processing. Apple and Google sign-in are included in the current beta; Facebook is limited to registered Meta app testers. Choose a provider on the account screen: your first sign-in creates your account automatically, and the same option signs you back in later. A saved session keeps you signed in between visits, including when listening offline. Signing out returns you to the account screen. Shared listening links still play in a browser without an account. RevenueCat manages subscription information. Cadenza does not use your information for advertising tracking.

Settings → Delete account removes your server account, cloud recordings, and listening links and requests cleanup of your sign-in provider connection and RevenueCat customer record. Local music remains on your device. Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple subscription.
