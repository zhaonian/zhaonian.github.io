A personal listening room

Music, made personal.

A piano nocturne for a quiet evening. A string quartet for a new beginning. Original classical music, shaped by your imagination.

Cadenza is a native iPhone and iPad app inspired by the expressive worlds of Beethoven, Chopin, Debussy, and Bach. Choose a mood and ensemble, give your piece a little direction, and keep your recordings in a private library on your device.

Cadenza is in development. The review build includes an original piano preview. Live composition and subscriptions are not yet available, and no public TestFlight or App Store download is available yet.

A little help, when you need it.

For playback, account, purchase, or privacy questions, contact the developer. Include your app version, device model, and a short description of what happened. Please do not send passwords, Apple verification codes, payment-card details, or private musical prompts.

Your recordings, close at hand.

Saved pieces play offline. You can rename, favorite, delete, or export a recording from your library. There is no library sync between devices in this version. Export music you want to keep before removing the app or replacing your device.

AI compositions are new generated recordings, not performances by the named composers. Generated music may resemble other outputs, and style or duration requests may not be followed exactly.

About Cadenza Patron.

Patron is planned to include 40 compositions per calendar month, available through monthly or annual subscriptions. Both plans use the same monthly allowance, which refreshes on the first day of the month at 00:00 UTC; unused compositions do not roll over. Three introductory compositions are planned for new accounts.

When subscriptions become available, the app will display the localized price and billing period before you confirm a purchase with Apple. Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled in Apple Account settings. Cancellation stops future renewals; access continues through the paid period. Saved recordings stay on your device after cancellation.

Use Settings → Restore purchases in Cadenza with the Apple Account used to purchase. Manage or cancel through Apple subscriptions. For refunds, visit Apple’s Report a Problem.

Privacy, with a clear choice.

Composing requires Sign in with Apple and permission to send your musical description to Google Lyria. Listening to the bundled preview does not require signing in. RevenueCat manages subscription information. Cadenza has no advertising trackers.

Once accounts are available, Settings → Delete account will remove your server account and temporary recordings and request cleanup of your Apple sign-in authorization and RevenueCat customer record. Local music remains on your device. Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple subscription.
