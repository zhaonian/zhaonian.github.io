Your photos and choices

Poke helps you choose photos to keep, discard, or favorite, compare bursts and nearby moments, and optionally save keepers to a named Apple Photos album. You retain ownership of your photos. Only use photos you are authorized to access and manage.

Keepers remain in Apple Photos. Creating or selecting an album is optional and does not create additional photo copies. A swipe marks a decision inside Poke; library changes happen when you finish review and apply them. Check your choices before confirming. Apple Photos separately confirms deletion and manages Recently Deleted and iCloud synchronization.

Poke is not a backup service. Keep backups of important memories. Photo grouping helps you compare similar moments; it is not a guarantee that every duplicate will be found. Storage savings are estimates, and space may not be released until Apple Photos permanently removes deleted items.

Free use and Poke Plus

The free plan includes up to 10 new library photos per calendar day on your installation, with all review tools included. Sample trips, undo, and reviewing the same photo again that day do not use additional allowance. Poke Plus removes the daily review limit while the subscription is active.

Plus is offered as a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription. The purchase screen displays your current localized price and billing period before purchase. Apple charges your Apple Account when you confirm. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting Poke does not cancel a subscription. Restore purchases in Poke’s settings using the Apple Account that purchased Plus. Cancellation normally leaves access active through the paid period, subject to Apple’s refund or revocation decisions. For billing and refund requests, use Apple’s purchase support; eligibility is determined by Apple and applicable law.
