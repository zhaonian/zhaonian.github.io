Lyrae is developed by Zhaonian Luan. The app is licensed under Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. This page explains Lyrae’s service and subscription rules. For assistance, contact jerry@zluan.io.

Creating and listening.

You must be 18 or older to use Lyrae’s music creation service. Sign-in is required in the app; shared listening links work without an account. Creation requires an internet connection and a supported region. Once accepted, a request runs in the cloud and the finished recording is saved to your private library. Failed creation requests return your composition credit. Already downloaded music can be played offline.

Your first three compositions are complimentary. Eligible new subscribers may receive a three-day free trial. The trial shares the same three-composition allowance with complimentary creations already used; starting a trial does not add three more credits.

Patron subscriptions.

Monthly and annual Patron subscriptions each include 40 compositions per calendar month during paid access. The allowance resets on the first day of each month at 00:00 UTC. Unused compositions do not roll over. Paid creations may receive generated cover art; complimentary and trial creations use predefined artwork. A cover-generation failure does not remove a finished recording.

The app shows the price, billing period, and any eligible trial before Apple asks you to confirm a purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled. A free trial converts to the selected paid subscription unless canceled before it ends; Apple recommends canceling at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid renewal. Manage or cancel through Apple Account subscriptions. Use Restore purchases in Lyrae with the same Apple Account. Deleting your Lyrae account does not cancel a subscription. For refund requests, use Apple’s Report a Problem.

Saved recordings remain available after subscription cancellation. Service interruptions, capacity limits, or regional restrictions may temporarily prevent creation. Contact support if a paid feature is unavailable.

Your music and the community.

Music is generated by AI. Results, length, and musical characteristics can vary. Outputs may resemble other generated works; Lyrae does not promise uniqueness, exclusive rights, or copyright protection. Only submit material you have the right to use. Composer inspiration describes musical influence, not a recording, performance, or endorsement by that person.

Pieces start private. Creating a listening link lets anyone with that link listen. Publishing adds a piece to public discovery. You can stop sharing or delete a piece. Copies someone already downloaded cannot be recalled.

Do not publish unlawful, abusive, discriminatory, threatening, sexually exploitative, misleading, or infringing content, or another person’s private information. Do not manipulate likes or create accounts to evade service limits. Report an item in the app or email support with its link. We may remove violating public content or restrict accounts. You may contact support to request review of a moderation decision.

Privacy and support.

The privacy policy explains sign-in, generation providers, cloud storage, sharing, and deletion. Do not include sensitive or confidential information in prompts. Send questions or content concerns to jerry@zluan.io.
