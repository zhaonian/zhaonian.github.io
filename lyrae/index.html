Your imagination, set to music

Begin with something
only you could imagine.

A quiet piano on a rainy afternoon. Strings with a sense of wonder. An orchestra for a new beginning.

Describe a feeling, scene or memory, and Lyrae uses AI to compose an original classical piece. No musical training needed. Follow your own idea, or take a little inspiration from a classical composer.

01

Make it yours.

Choose piano, strings or orchestra. Shape the mood and tempo, with an approximate length of one to three minutes. Keep the details simple, or explore the studio settings.

02

Leave room to listen.

Once your piece is accepted in the cloud, you can close the app. Allow notifications when you first compose to hear when it is ready. Delivery depends on your device settings and connection.

03

Keep what moves you.

Save recordings for offline listening. Keep them private, publish them in Listen, or send a listening link that plays in a browser. Discover studio selections and pieces liked by real listeners.

Lyrae Patron

A little more room to create.

Start with up to 3 complimentary compositions. Eligible subscribers can try Patron free for 3 days, sharing that same three-composition allowance. Starting a trial does not refill credits already used.

Paid membership includes 40 compositions per UTC calendar month, plus original AI cover art for new pieces. Unused compositions do not roll over. Free and trial pieces use curated artwork. Listening remains available without a subscription.

US plans: $7.99/month or $79.99/year. The app shows your local price and offer eligibility before purchase. Plans renew automatically unless canceled in Apple Account settings. Saved recordings stay after cancellation.

Contact support
I already have Cadenza.

Cadenza is becoming Lyrae. Your existing account, recordings and subscriptions carry over. Keep using the same sign-in option. Existing shared music links continue to work.

How do I find my music on another device?

Sign in with the same Apple or Google account and provider. Your private cloud library returns when you connect; download recordings to play them offline. Apple and Google sign-in are supported. Facebook sign-in is currently unavailable.

How do I manage my subscription?

Use Settings → Restore purchases in the app. Manage or cancel in Apple Account subscriptions. Request refunds through Apple’s Report a Problem. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription.

What stays private?

New pieces start private. You choose what to publish or share. Public profiles show the name and handle you choose, not your sign-in identity. Your musical prompt is not shown on public listening pages. Read our privacy policy for the details.

Sign-in is required in the app. Cloud features need internet access and are subject to service capacity. AI compositions are generated recordings, not performances by the named composers; requested styles and lengths may vary.
