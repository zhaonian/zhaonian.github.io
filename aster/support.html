A little help,
when you need it.

Email lzn0830@gmail.com with your Aster version, iOS version, and a description of what happened. Please don’t include private saved content or payment details.

Put Aster at the front of Share

Open any iOS share sheet → swipe the app row to More → Edit → add Aster to Favorites → drag Aster to the top → Done. iOS and your Favorites control the order.

A post has no details

Some social posts are private, expired, or restricted. Your link stays saved. Add a caption or screenshot, then try finding details again. Aster does not invent unavailable information.

Apple Intelligence is unavailable

Check that your device supports Apple Intelligence, uses iOS 26 or later, has the models enabled and downloaded, and uses a supported language. Source extraction and ordinary saving remain available without AI.

Manage or restore Plus

Open Aster settings → Restore purchases using the purchasing Apple Account. Manage or cancel from Apple subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a plan. Existing items remain readable after cancellation.

My shared finds are waiting

The free plan holds 30 saved items. Additional shares stay safely queued. Delete an item to make room or activate Plus, then open Aster to finish importing.

Exporting and backups

Settings → Export full library creates JSON with text, details, and private notes. Photos remain on the device. Aster does not sync across devices in this version.
